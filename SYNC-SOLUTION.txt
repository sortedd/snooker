localStorage is browser-specific. Each device/browser has its own
separate storage. This is by design for security and privacy.

- Desktop Chrome: Has localStorage A
- Mobile Safari: Has localStorage B  
- Incognito mode: Has localStorage C (empty)

They CANNOT share data without a server or cloud storage.
